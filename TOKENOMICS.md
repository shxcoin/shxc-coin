# SHXC Coin — Tokenomics & Supply

## Standard allocation model
For standard 100,000,000,000 SHXC deployments:

| Allocation | Percentage | Amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Market & Liquidity | 70% | 70,000,000,000 SHXC |
| Project Treasury | 30% | 30,000,000,000 SHXC |

Do not mechanically apply this allocation to deployments created under different network or launch-platform constraints without network-specific confirmation.

## Multichain supply
See [NETWORKS.md](NETWORKS.md). The aggregate of published network-specific supplies is **1,516,021,000,000 SHXC-equivalent units**, provided only as a transparency reference.

## Administrative commerce rate
Within supported S.H SmartHub commerce workflows:

**1 SHXC = 0.000001 USD**

This is not a stablecoin peg, guaranteed market price, guaranteed valuation, redemption promise, or liquidity guarantee.

## Legacy SHX
SHX is a historical/discontinued predecessor. SHXC is the current ecosystem token. No automatic SHX-to-SHXC migration should be inferred unless separately published through official channels.
